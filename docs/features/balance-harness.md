# The Balance Harness

The kit's proof of depth — and your tuning tool.

## What it does

Automated run-throughs play the game without a human and output **win-rate curves per act and per deck archetype**. The output is a recorded artifact (CSV plus chart data) in the verification record — real output, not a screenshot of a claim.

## Why the curves are comparable

- **One documented policy.** The automated player follows a written policy, and the same policy runs for every archetype — so differences in the curves are differences in the content, not in the pilot.
- **Reproducible.** The same seed set produces the same curves within a recorded tolerance.
- **Every shipped card is played.** The harness plays each of the 150+ cards at least once and asserts zero exceptions, and every archetype's win rate is above zero — every card appears in at least one winnable run.

## Your content joins in

A card you add as data appears in the harness's card pool and its win-rate contribution is reported. A test enemy added as data appears in the encounter pool. Balance your expansion the same way the kit was balanced.
