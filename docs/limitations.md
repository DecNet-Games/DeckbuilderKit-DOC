# Limitations — what this kit is not

Stated plainly, so you can judge the fit before you buy:

- **No networking, no multiplayer, no online play.** Single-player only, by deliberate scope — no netcode, no shared-run server, no PvP.
- **No art packs.** Card art is flat, minimal, clearly labelled placeholder, and reskinnable — it demonstrates the game, it is not an art library. No 3D models, no character art.
- **No custom rendering.** No custom shaders, no Shader Graph systems, no VFX packs.
- **The harness measures balance; it does not design for you.** Win-rate curves tell you where your content stands under the documented policy. Making the cards interesting remains the design work — yours, starting week one instead of month three.
- **One product, one edition.** There is no second tier or add-on module at launch; what is documented here is what ships.
